# Features
features:
  - title: "Why attend XAI-Ed 2027?"
    image: "/images/why_attend.png"
    content: >-
      The 2025 revision of the definition of learning analytics places the
      interpretation and communication of data about learners alongside its
      collection and analysis, and names theoretically relevant and actionable
      insights that enhance learning and teaching as the purpose of the field.
      Explainability is the mechanism through which that interpretation and
      communication happen. Yet evidence about what explanations accomplish in
      education is still thin. This edition of XAI-Ed asks two questions: how
      explanations affect learning processes and outcomes, and how they become
      actionable for students, teachers, parents and caregivers, and
      educational leaders. Equity, fairness, and transparency run through both.
