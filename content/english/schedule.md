### Programme Schedule

XAI-Ed 2027 is a full-day workshop for up to 40 participants. Both design activities work from a scenario pack distributed in advance, so that groups working on the same material for different stakeholders produce comparable artefacts.

| Time | Activity | Type of Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 09:00–09:20 | Welcome and framing | Presentation |
| 09:20–10:00 | [Invited talk on explainability and learning outcomes]({{< relref "keynote" >}}), followed by open discussion | Talk |
| 10:00–10:45 | Paper session 1: explanation, learning processes, and outcomes | Paper session |
| 10:45–11:15 | Coffee break | Break |
| 11:15–12:30 | Design activity 1: *the explanation clinic*. Groups redesign one scenario for an assigned stakeholder, producing an explanation design card | Group activity |
| 12:30–13:30 | Lunch | Break |
| 13:30–14:15 | Paper session 2 and demonstration carousel: actionability, fairness, and Gen-AI | Paper session & demos |
| 14:15–15:00 | [Cross-stakeholder panel: what counts as evidence that an explanation worked?]({{< relref "panel" >}}) | Discussion |
| 15:00–15:30 | Coffee break | Break |
| 15:30–16:30 | Design activity 2: *tracing the explanation-to-action chain*. Groups exchange cards, locate where the chain breaks, and turn each failure point into a research question | Group activity |
| 16:30–17:00 | Synthesis of the research agenda, outputs, and community continuation | Discussion |
